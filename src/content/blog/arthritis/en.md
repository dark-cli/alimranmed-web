  - type: prose
    heading: "Types of Arthritis"
    body: |
      Arthritis comes in many forms:
      
      - Idiopathic arthritis
      - Spondylitis
      - Septic arthritis
      - Reactive arthritis
      - Rheumatoid arthritis
      - Psoriatic arthritis
      - Thumb arthritis
      - Osteoarthritis (bone wear)
      - Gout
      - Joint infection
      
      The two most common are Osteoarthritis and Rheumatoid arthritis. Osteoarthritis causes cartilage damage—the hard and slippery tissue covering bone ends—leading to joint deformation. Rheumatoid arthritis is an autoimmune disorder attacking the synovial membrane (joint lining), causing inflammation and eventual cartilage and bone damage.
      
      Less common types may result from other conditions like lupus (affecting kidneys, lungs and joints) or psoriasis.
  - type: prose
    heading: "Causes"
    body: |
      **Osteoarthritis** develops from wear and tear on joint cartilage—the smooth layer covering bone ends. Severe damage causes bone-on-bone grinding, restricting movement and causing pain. This wear accumulates over years, or joint injuries and inflammation can accelerate it.
      
      **Rheumatoid arthritis** occurs when the immune system attacks the joint lining (synovial membrane). This powerful membrane becomes inflamed and swollen, eventually damaging both cartilage and bone within the joint.
  - type: prose
    heading: "Symptoms"
    body: |
      Common joint-related symptoms include:
      
      - Soreness
      - Stiffness
      - Swelling
      - Redness
      - Decreased range of motion
      
      Some arthritis types cause systemic symptoms:
      
      - Fever
      - Fatigue
      - Rash
      - Weight loss
      - Breathing problems
      - Dry eyes and mouth
      - Mouth ulcers
      - Mood changes
  - type: prose
    heading: "Risk Factors"
    body: |
      - **Genetics**: Arthritis often runs in families, making you vulnerable to triggering factors
      - **Age**: Risk increases with aging, especially for osteoarthritis, rheumatoid arthritis and gout
      - **Gender**: Rheumatoid arthritis is more common in women; gout is more common in men
      - **Previous joint injury**: Earlier joint injuries (sports or trauma) increase arthritis risk in that joint
      - **Obesity**: Excess weight strains weight-bearing joints (knees, hips, spine), raising arthritis risk
  - type: prose
    heading: "Complications"
    body: |
      Severe arthritis can make daily tasks difficult, especially when affecting hands or arms. Arthritis in weight-bearing joints may prevent comfortable walking or sitting. In advanced cases, joints can become twisted and permanently deformed.
