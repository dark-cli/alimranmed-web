  - type: prose
    heading: "What Causes Tension Headaches"
    body: |
      The exact mechanism of tension headaches is not fully understood. Several factors are thought to be involved, including genetics and environment. Muscle contractions in the head and neck are considered a major factor in their development. Many people experience tension headaches in response to stressful events or hectic days.
  - type: prose
    heading: "Symptoms of Tension Headaches"
    body: |
      Common symptoms include:
      
      - Slow, gradual onset of pain
      - Pain usually affects both sides of the head
      - Dull pain or sensation of a band or vice around the head
      - Pain may involve the back of the head or neck
      - Pain is typically mild to moderate (not severe)
      
      Since symptoms can resemble other conditions, always consult your healthcare provider for proper diagnosis.
  - type: prose
    heading: "Lifestyle Management"
    body: |
      Good headache management depends on reducing stress and tension. Key strategies include:
      
      - Maintaining consistent sleep schedule (same bedtime and wake time)
      - Exercising regularly for at least 30 minutes daily
      - Eating regular meals (don’t skip breakfast)
      - Avoiding known headache triggers (certain foods, lack of sleep)
      - Resting in a quiet, dark environment when needed
      - Stress management through yoga, massage, and relaxation exercises
      - Taking medications as recommended by your healthcare provider
