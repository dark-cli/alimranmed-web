    heading: "TMS in Migraine Treatment"
    body: |
      [Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS)](/services/brain-stimulation/tms/) is a non-invasive brain stimulation method used worldwide to assess brain-behavior interactions, cortical reactivity, and map functionally relevant brain regions. TMS induces controlled current pulses in specific cortical areas and is an effective technology with diagnostic and therapeutic uses in various diseases.
      
      Clinical applications show promising results in treating psychiatric and neurological conditions, including migraine. In migraine patients, treatment is divided into urgent/abortive treatment (stopping acute attacks) and prophylactic treatment (prevention).
      
      First-line medications typically include simple analgesics and non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs. However, many patients have attacks that don't respond to various medications or develop medication overuse headache. Among non-pharmacologic therapies, TMS has been extensively studied as a preventive migraine treatment with excellent outcome results.
