  - type: prose
    heading: "Understanding Tinnitus"
    body: |
      Tinnitus is disturbing noise heard without any hearing stimulus that affects quality of life and leads to psychosocial problems. Its prevalence increases with aging—seen in 33% of the general population. The condition accompanies nearly all auditory system disorders, but its pathophysiology is not fully understood, making evaluation and treatment challenging.
  - type: prose
    heading: "TMS as a Treatment Solution"
    body: |
      Despite treatment challenges, progress in auditory neuroscience provides positive perspectives on tinnitus management. [Transcranial magnetic stimulation (TMS)](/services/brain-stimulation/tms/) is a non-invasive method stimulating neuronal tissue without requiring electrical current transfer through electrodes or skin contact.
      
      TMS is used for treating various neurological diseases with advancing neuroscience. In recent years, studies on low-frequency repetitive TMS for tinnitus treatment have increased significantly, with most showing successful results. Repetitive TMS for tinnitus represents a novel and promising therapeutic approach.
