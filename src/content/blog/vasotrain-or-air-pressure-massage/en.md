  - type: prose
    heading: "How it works"
    body: |
      Vasotrain massages the affected limb through air pressure at varying levels, delivered by a programmable sleeve. The programme is chosen for the specific condition and adjusted to the patient's diagnosis.

      The device sits inside a wider rehabilitation plan — used alongside conventional [physiotherapy](/services/physiotherapy/physical-therapy/), not as a substitute for it.

  - type: prose
    heading: "When we use it"
    body: |
      Vasotrain is included in the treatment plan for a range of conditions where circulation, mobility, or targeted limb massage form part of recovery:

      - Swelling of the upper and lower limbs after lymphoedema, particularly post-mastectomy cases
      - Poor blood circulation in patients with diabetes
      - Mobility challenges in patients with paraplegia or quadriplegia
      - [Stroke](/treatments/stroke/) patients as part of the rehabilitation programme
      - Patients with osteoporosis
      - Recovery following cast removal in fracture cases
      - Back and limb pain in patients with a slipped disc
