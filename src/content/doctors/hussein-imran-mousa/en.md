## Weekly Schedule

Working as a highly engaged neurosurgeon, ten hours per day, with a weekly schedule as follows:

- One day as consultant surgeon participating in the on-call roster (out-of-hours emergency call).
- One day per week in the operating theatre for various neurosurgical cases: tumours, injuries, congenital malformations, spine, orbital surgery and others. Areas of focus: endoscopic neurosurgery, spine surgery and pain interventions.
- One day for training trainers from various specialties and medical grades, and different health professions and occupational groups, clinically and theoretically in groups on the management of critical cases and Advanced Life Support.
- One day for outpatient consultations to receive various referrals from hospitals, health centres and private clinics related to neurosurgery.
- One day for outpatient consultations at Basra Specialised Oncology Centre to receive tumours related to the nervous system and intractable pain requiring control.
- One day for pain interventions.

## Pain Management Interventions

Given growing interest in pain management, we perform various interventions for controlling intractable pain, including:

1. Regional blocks
2. Neurolytic procedures
3. Radiofrequency ablation
4. Sympathetic blocks, including:
    - Coeliac ganglion
    - Ganglion impar
    - Thoracic sympathetic
    - Stellate ganglion
    - Superior hypogastric plexus
    - Lumbar sympathetic
5. Sacroiliac joint injections
6. Caudal epidural injections
7. Median nerve injections
8. Facet joint injections
9. Interlaminar epidural injections
10. Transforaminal (perineural) epidural injections
