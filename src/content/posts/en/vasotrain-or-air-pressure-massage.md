It is an alternative method and assistance in medical rehabilitation and physical therapy programs  
Its work is based on the principle of massage for the affected part through varying air pressure and the choice of the appropriate program according to the diagnosis of the case.

Some of the most important cases that are included in the treatment:  
Swelling of the upper and lower extremities after lumphedema, especially cases of mastectomy.  
Poor circulation of blood circulation in diabetics.  
Inability to walk for patients with paraplec or quadriplegia.  
Stroke patients.  
Bone fragility patients.  
After opening the splint in cases of fractures.  
Back and limb pain in spinal skid patients
