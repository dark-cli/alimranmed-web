![](/images/legacy/2020/07/1-s2.0-S1388245718311052-gr1-221x300.jpg)Recently, clinicians have been using repetitive [transcranial magnetic stimulation (rTMS)](/services/brain-stimulation/tms/) for treating various pain conditions. Overall, our findings suggested that rTMS is beneficial for treating neuropathic pain of various origins, such as central pain, pain from peripheral nerve disorders, fibromyalgia, and migraine. Although data on the use of rTMS for orofacial pain, including trigeminal neuralgia, phantom pain, low back pain, myofascial pain syndrome, pelvic pain, and complex regional pain syndrome, were promising, there was insufficient evidence to determine the efficacy of rTMS for treating these conditions.
