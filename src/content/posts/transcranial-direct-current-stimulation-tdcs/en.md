Transcranial direct current stimulation (tDCS) is a wearable, portable brain stimulation technology that delivers a low electrical current to the scalp. It is a neuromodulation technique that produces immediate and lasting changes in brain function. The positions of the anode (positive) and cathode (negative) electrodes on the head determine how current flows to specific areas of the brain. The current delivered by tDCS is not strong enough to trigger an action potential in a neuron. Instead, this sub-threshold current changes the electrical activity pattern of the neurons. The idea is that while the brain is active, for example while a person is trying to do something or learn it, tDCS reinforces this ongoing activity at the cellular level. tDCS alters the firing of neurons and enhances synaptic transmission between neurons by increasing synaptic plasticity.

What does a tDCS device look like?  
Small, battery-powered devices. There is a control panel that allows you to program the device (to set the duration and intensity of stimulation). The electrodes are placed on the head and held in place by a head strap, usually a rubber band, and the cable of each electrode is attached to the stimulator.  
When the device is turned on, current flows from the device to the electrode and then across the brain.  
Our professional-grade stimulators have many features that help ensure effective stimulation and reliability, including an impedance meter and an ammeter.

What are the side effects of transcranial direct current stimulation?  
The side effects reported so far are minor, limited to the site of the electrode, and include:  
Temporary redness of the skin  
Itching  
Twitching  
Headache  
Nausea  
Dizziness

It should be noted that these last three side effects have been reported to occur at approximately the same rate as with placebo (sham) stimulation.

How does tDCS feel?  
People feel a slight tingling, itching or warmth. These sensations are not painful and disappear when the stimulation stops.

What are the benefits of tDCS?  
tDCS is used for many different applications that involve changing the brain to affect how people think or feel:  
Depression  
Schizophrenia  
Aphasia  
Addiction  
Memory enhancement

tDCS has been shown to help people learn faster, and it is also used for accelerated learning, focus, relaxation and meditation.  
Can tDCS treat anxiety?  
tDCS has been shown to reduce anxiety in several clinical trials at leading medical centers.

Can tDCS treat depression?  
Several clinical trials have reported that tDCS can treat depression, and it has fewer side effects than drugs.
