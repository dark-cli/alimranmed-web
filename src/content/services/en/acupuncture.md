## Overview

Acupuncture is a very old therapeutic technique of traditional Chinese medicine using thin needles for pain relief and treating various conditions.

## Claimed Benefits

Acupuncture is presented as a powerful therapeutic alternative to analgesics that patients may find relaxing and comfortable. It works by stimulating blood circulation and triggering the body's natural pain-relieving mechanisms.

## Conditions Treated

- Chronic pain (back, neck, joint-related)
- Headaches and migraines
- Arthritis and osteoarthritis
- Post-operative pain
- Musculoskeletal disorders
- Digestive issues and anxiety
- Insomnia and fatigue
- Allergies
- Support for smoking cessation and weight loss
