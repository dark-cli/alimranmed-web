## Overview

BOTOX (Botulinum Toxin Type A) is a protein produced by the bacteria *Clostridium botulinum* that functions by blocking nerve signals to reduce muscle spasms when administered in small doses.

## How It Works

The treatment operates by interrupting communication between nerves and muscles, preventing or minimizing involuntary muscle contractions. This approach has been employed clinically since the early 1990s.

## Conditions Treated

BOTOX injections are commonly used for patients experiencing:

- Headaches
- Myofascial pain
- Fibromyalgia
- Chronic low back pain
- Post-stroke spasticity
