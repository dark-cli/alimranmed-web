## What is tDCS?

Transcranial direct current stimulation (tDCS) is a brain stimulation technology that delivers low electrical current to the scalp through portable, battery-powered devices. Rather than triggering neuron firing, it modulates electrical patterns at the cellular level to enhance synaptic activity.

## Device Features

The equipment consists of small battery-powered units with control panels for programming stimulation duration and intensity. Electrodes attach to the scalp via a head cover, with professional-grade devices including impedance meters for reliability monitoring.

## Sensations and Safety

Users typically experience mild tingling or warmth that disappears after stimulation ends. Reported side effects are minor, limited to the site of the electrode and include temporary skin redness, itching, and occasional headache or dizziness — occurring at similar rates as placebo treatments.

## Clinical Applications

tDCS is used to address:

- Depression and anxiety
- Schizophrenia
- Cognitive enhancement
- Memory improvement
- Aphasia and addiction

Research indicates it can reduce anxiety in several clinical trials and treat depression with fewer side effects than drugs.
