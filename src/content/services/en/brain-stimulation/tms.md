## Overview

Alimran Medical Center offers TMS, a non-invasive brain stimulation therapy. The TMS device is made of one or two copper coils, positioned on an external, targeted area of a patient's scalp, which produces brief magnetic pulses to an estimated depth of approximately 2 to 2.5 centimeters.

## Clinical Applications

The facility uses TMS to treat various conditions, including:

- Parkinson's Disease
- Depression and mood disorders
- Neuropathic pain conditions
- Alzheimer's disease
- Addiction disorders
- Tinnitus
- Stroke recovery
- Epilepsy

## Treatment Details

Sessions typically last 30–40 minutes, conducted five days weekly over 4–6 weeks. Patients are fully awake and aware during the treatment and require no anesthesia or hospitalization.

## Side Effects

The most common adverse effect is mild headaches, affecting roughly half of patients. The risk of a seizure is exceedingly low — one in 1,000 patients experience a provoked seizure.

## Contraindications

Patients with non-removable metal implants cannot receive TMS, including aneurysm clips, deep brain stimulators, or shrapnel in the head.
