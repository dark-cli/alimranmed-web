## SPINMED Overview

SpineMED is a non-surgical spinal decompression device designed to treat disc-related pain conditions. The technology addresses back and neck pain by relieving pressure on damaged vertebral discs, allowing fluids, nutrients and oxygen to flow back into the disc.

## Treatment Mechanism

The device works through a sensing system that monitors applied stress every 2.5 seconds and adjusts tension every 20 seconds. This distinguishes it from traditional traction devices by preventing involuntary muscle contractions during treatment.

## Treatment Applications

SpineMED addresses multiple conditions:

- Disc herniation and slippage
- Failed back surgery syndrome
- Nerve inflammation from vertebral pressure
- Chronic pain lasting beyond four weeks

## Treatment Protocol

A typical course involves 20–25 sessions lasting 30 minutes each, conducted 3–5 times weekly over 4–6 weeks. Sessions are described as safe and comfortable, with patients able to relax while listening to music or watching films.
