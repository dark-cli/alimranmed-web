## Overview

Mesotherapy is a non-surgical body contouring procedure designed to dissolve localized fat deposits beneath the skin. The treatment employs micro-needles to inject active compounds directly into fatty tissue layers.

## How It Works

The procedure relies on a strong substance capable of dissolving the fatty tissue accumulated under the skin. The active ingredients typically consist of enzymes, vitamins, amino acids, minerals, and some medicinal drugs that break down fat cells, which the body then naturally eliminates.

## Safety Profile

A key advantage over surgical alternatives is that mesotherapy is not a surgical procedure. Therefore, there are no surgical risks or complications that may come with liposuction procedures.

## Treatment Results

Most patients require between 5 to 10 sessions, though the exact number depends on individual circumstances and fat volume. Results can be permanent with proper weight maintenance — results are permanent if you maintain your health and weight as much as possible.

## Common Applications

- Targeted fat reduction in specific body areas (abdomen, thighs, arms, face)
- Cellulite treatment
- Neck and double chin refinement
- Skin tightening and anti-aging effects
- Post-surgical deformity correction
