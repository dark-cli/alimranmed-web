## Overview

Alimran Medical Center offers ozone therapy, a treatment method discovered in the 1840s that uses a mixture of oxygen and ozone rather than pure ozone.

## Key Claimed Benefits

- Liver detoxification and lipid breakdown
- Improved cell metabolism and blood flow
- Reduction of uric acid levels
- Enhanced oxygen supply to tissues
- Antimicrobial properties against 99% of bacteria, viruses and fungi

## Treatment Applications

The center lists approximately 20 conditions treated with ozone therapy, including:

- Cardiovascular issues (coronary calcification, post-heart attack recovery)
- Rheumatoid disorders and sciatica
- Stroke follow-up treatment
- Liver damage and hepatitis
- Skin conditions and eye diseases
- Migraine and vertigo

## Treatment Protocol

A typical therapy cycle consists of 10 to 12 treatments. Early-stage intervention is emphasised for optimal results, and ozone therapy can complement other biological medicine approaches.
