## Overview

Alimran Medical Center offers regenerative medicine, also referred to as **Non-Surgical Ligament and Tendon Reconstruction**. This approach targets joint instability by stimulating the body's natural healing processes.

## How It Works

The procedure functions by stimulating the body's natural healing mechanisms to lay down new tissue in the weakened area through directed injections. This mild inflammatory response encourages growth of new ligament or tendon fibers, gradually rebuilding tissue strength.

## Treatment Solutions

The injections contain natural substances that stimulate the healing response, as well as local anesthetic agents. Formulations may include dextrose, saline, sarapin, procaine, lidocaine, platelet-rich plasma (PRP), or autologous adult stem cells from bone marrow or adipose tissue.

## Treatable Conditions

- Back and neck pain
- Knee problems and meniscal tears
- Shoulder pain and rotator cuff tears
- Elbow conditions (tennis/golfer's elbow)
- Ankle instability
- Osteoarthritis
- Plantar fasciitis
- Temporomandibular joint (TMJ) syndrome

Evaluation by experienced physicians is essential for accurate assessment and individualised treatment planning.
