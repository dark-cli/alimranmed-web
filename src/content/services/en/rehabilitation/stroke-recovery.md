Alimran Medical Center in Basra, Iraq offers comprehensive stroke rehabilitation services. Stroke is one of the leading causes of disability in Iraq, and the center emphasises the importance of multidisciplinary rehabilitation approaches.

## Core Rehabilitation Philosophy

The center employs a team-based model incorporating medical oversight, nursing support, physical therapy, occupational therapy, speech therapy, respiratory care, nutrition planning, and psychological support. Primary aims include neurological recovery through neuroplasticity, functional restoration via targeted training, and enhanced quality of life through emotional wellness.

## Key Recovery Phases

The center prioritises interventions during the critical **Golden Period** (first 90 days) through:

- Initial assessment and risk evaluation
- Collaborative goal-setting with families and care teams
- Continuous performance monitoring and care plan adjustments
- Patient empowerment and transition to structured rehabilitation settings

Stroke recovery outcomes vary by individual and require personalised, evidence-based care plans.
