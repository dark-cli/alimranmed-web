## Procedure Overview

Vertebroplasty is an outpatient surgical intervention designed to stabilize compression fractures affecting the spine. The procedure involves injecting bone cement into fractured or cracked vertebrae, which hardens to provide structural support.

## Key Benefits

The treatment offers several advantages for patients with severe, disabling pain from compression fractures:

- Pain relief
- Increased mobility
- Reduced dependence on pain medications

## Related Procedure

**Kyphoplasty** operates on similar principles but employs special balloons to create spaces within the vertebra before cement injection. This technique can correct spinal deformity and restore lost vertebral height.

## Common Cause

The fractures typically occur due to osteoporosis, a condition weakening bone density.
