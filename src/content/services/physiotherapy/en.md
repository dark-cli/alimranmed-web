Physiotherapy, or physical therapy, is a healthcare profession responsible for assessment, diagnosis, treatment, and prevention of diseases and disabilities through physical means. Physiotherapists are experts in movement and function who work in partnership with their patients, helping them overcome movement disorders that may have been present from birth, acquired through an accident or injury, or the result of ageing or changing life events.

**![](https://ar.alimranmed.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/تنزيل-25.jpg)**

The main role of physiotherapy is to focus on restoring functional movement after injury, illness, or even disability that has negatively affected your mobility. The physiotherapist works as part of a multidisciplinary team, working alongside doctors and nurses to complement treatments. There are several physiotherapy methods, and it is preferable to use more than one method at the same time, or they may be used together with other therapeutic methods or after surgery to increase effectiveness.

Physiotherapy is considered safer and has fewer side effects than other therapeutic methods. Pharmaceutical drugs involve introducing chemical substances that the body considers foreign to achieve the same goal, but these substances alter metabolism within body function and can interact, thereby causing more potential side effects.

**Common Physiotherapy Modalities:**

1.  Massage
2.  Heat or cold therapy
3.  Electrical stimulation
4.  Ultrasound
5.  Laser
6.  Shortwave
7.  Infrared
8.  Long waves
9.  Microwave
10.  Therapeutic exercises

**General Benefits of Physiotherapy:**

![](https://ar.alimranmed.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/images-37.jpg)

1.  Relieves pain
2.  Helps increase blood flow, which reduces inflammation
3.  Deactivates trigger points responsible for myofascial pain
4.  Increases the specific speed strength and tensile capacity to repair damaged tissue
5.  Increases blood circulation activity
6.  Stimulates nerve function
7.  Develops collagen and muscle tissue
8.  Helps generate new healthy cellular tissue
9.  "Wakes up" weak or inactive muscles
10.  Helps improve muscle strength and endurance
11.  Helps improve muscle-brain pathways
12.  Assists with joint movements to help associated muscles work better

Specifically, physiotherapy improves the client's quality of life by:

-   Encouraging optimal mobility, physical activity, and overall health and wellness.
-   Preventing illness, injury, and disability.
-   Managing acute and chronic conditions, activity limitations, and participation restrictions.
-   Improving and maintaining optimal functional independence and physical performance.
-   Rehabilitating injuries and the effects of disease or disability with therapeutic exercise programs and other interventions.
-   Educating and planning maintenance and support programs to prevent re-occurrence, re-injury, or functional decline.

![](https://ar.alimranmed.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/images-36-300x168.jpg)

Many people think physiotherapy is used only for the musculoskeletal system. But in fact, physiotherapy is both preventive and rehabilitative for a range of mobility issues, injuries, and/or diseases.

The following list is only some of many different areas where physiotherapy can help:

-   Arthritis
-   Asthma
-   Back pain
-   Cancer
-   Cardiovascular conditions (including post-heart attack, chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD), and pneumonia)
-   Cerebral palsy
-   Chronic pain
-   Tremors
-   Critical care
-   Dementia
-   Developmental delay and range of childhood conditions and care
-   Diabetes
-   Falls and fractures
-   High blood pressure
-   Urinary incontinence
-   Multiple sclerosis
-   Neck pain
-   Neurological conditions (stroke, tremors, spinal cord injury, Parkinson's disease)
-   Occupational health
-   Oncology-related conditions (including lymphedema)
-   Osteoporosis
-   Pregnancy- and childbirth-related urinary incontinence
-   Rehabilitation
-   Vestibular disorders (dizziness)
