    body: |
      The physical therapy department at Alimran Medical Center provides a broad range of evidence-based treatment modalities for musculoskeletal and neurological rehabilitation. Modalities available include:

      [Electrical stimulation](/services/physiotherapy/electrical-stimulation/) — TENS and interferential therapy for chronic pain relief, muscle activation, and oedema reduction.

      [Laser therapy](/services/physiotherapy/laser-therapy/) — low-level laser (photobioactivation) for tissue repair, inflammation reduction, and trigger point treatment.

      [Ultrasound therapy](/services/physiotherapy/ultrasound-therapy/) — therapeutic high-frequency sound waves for deep tissue healing, scar tissue flexibility, and pain reduction.

      [Luna EMG robot](/services/physiotherapy/luna-emg-robot/) — interactive electromyography-driven robotic rehabilitation for neurological and orthopaedic recovery.

      Treatments are frequently combined with each other and with complementary services such as radiofrequency, acupuncture, and chiropractic care for comprehensive patient management.
