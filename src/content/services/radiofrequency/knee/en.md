    heading: "Knee radiofrequency at Alimran Medical Center"
    body: |
      Chronic osteoarthritis pain of the knee is often inadequately managed with prescribed medications alone. Radiofrequency (RF) ablation applied to the articular nerve branches (genicular nerves) of the knee provides a therapeutic alternative for patients with chronic knee pain, offering meaningful and durable pain relief.

      Although surgery is generally effective for patients with advanced disease, older individuals or those with other chronic conditions may not be appropriate surgical candidates. Similarly, some patients prefer non-surgical options. For these patients, radiofrequency ablation of the genicular nerves is a successful and well-established alternative.

      The procedure is based on interrupting the nerve supply to the painful joint. By creating a radiofrequency heat lesion on the genicular nerve branches, the pain signal pathway is disrupted, providing relief that can last from several months to over a year.

      Indications include chronic knee pain secondary to osteoarthritis, failed knee replacement, patients unfit for knee replacement surgery, and patients who wish to avoid surgery.
