    heading: "Radiofrequency at Alimran Medical Center"
    body: |
      Radiofrequency neurotomy (also called radiofrequency ablation or lesioning) is a minimally invasive procedure that applies heat to nerve pathways to interrupt pain signal transmission to the brain. Multiple clinical studies show that RFA significantly reduces pain severity and frequency for 1 to 2 years in the majority of patients. More than 70% of patients experience meaningful pain relief.

      Two types are available: **Thermal Radiofrequency**, in which tissue is heated to up to 80°C, and **Pulsed Radiofrequency**, in which tissue temperature does not exceed 45°C — a gentler option used when thermal ablation is not appropriate.

      RFA is performed on an outpatient basis requiring only local anaesthetic and mild sedation, avoiding the risks of open surgery and general anaesthesia. Benefits include: up to 2 years of pain relief, less risk of side effects compared to steroid injections, low complication rate, improved range of motion, reduced analgesic use, and short recovery time.

      Preparation: fast for 6 hours before the procedure and arrange a driver. Post-procedure restrictions: no driving or machinery for 24 hours, no strenuous activity for 24 hours, avoid baths for 1–2 days (showering is permitted).
