  - type: prose
    heading: Endoscopic spine surgery at Alimran Medical Center
    body: >
      Full-endoscopic spine surgery uses a high-resolution endoscope to provide intraoperative imaging through minimised
      access ports. This approach implements minimally invasive surgical concepts while maintaining the precision
      required for spinal pathology.


      Three standardised surgical approaches are used depending on the location and type of pathology:


      **Transforaminal technique** — used for pathologies at L4/5 and higher segments. Lateral transforaminal access
      targets intraspinal and intraforaminal pathologies; posterolateral access targets intradiscal pathologies.


      **Extraforaminal technique** — used for extraforaminal disc herniations and foraminal stenosis at all lumbar and
      thoracic segments.


      **Interlaminar technique** — preferred for intraspinal pathologies at L5/S1 where the interlaminar window allows
      direct access without extensive bone removal.


      **Indications** include: disc herniation (direct neural decompression with minimal access trauma and selective
      discectomy to reduce recurrence risk), spinal canal stenosis (foraminal, lateral recess, and central), facet and
      sacroiliac joint syndrome, and diskogenic pain syndrome including nucleoplasty.
