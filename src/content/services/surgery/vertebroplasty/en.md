  - type: prose
    heading: Vertebroplasty at Alimran Medical Center
    body: >
      Vertebroplasty is an outpatient procedure for stabilising compression fractures of the spine. Bone cement is
      injected into cracked or broken vertebrae — most commonly caused by osteoporosis — where it hardens quickly,
      stabilising the fracture and supporting the spinal column.


      For patients suffering from severe, disabling pain caused by vertebral compression fractures, vertebroplasty can
      provide rapid and meaningful pain relief, increased mobility, and a significant reduction in pain medication use.


      **Kyphoplasty** is a related variation of the procedure that uses small inflatable balloons inserted into the
      vertebra to create a cavity before the cement is injected. This additional step can correct spinal deformity
      caused by the fracture and restore lost vertebral height — making kyphoplasty particularly useful for patients
      with significant kyphotic deformity or height loss.


      Both vertebroplasty and kyphoplasty are minimally invasive procedures performed under image guidance, avoiding the
      risks of open spinal surgery.
