title: Ankle pain
description: >-
  Ankle pain arises from injury to bones, ligaments, or tendons, or from arthritis. Mild cases often respond to home care;
  severe or post-injury pain needs medical assessment.
category: pain

sections:
  - type: highlights
    items:
      - label: What it is
        value: >-
          Pain in or around the ankle joint, which can involve the bones, ligaments, tendons, or muscles. It may occur on
          the inside, outside, or along the Achilles tendon at the back of the heel.
      - label: Symptoms
        value: >-
          Pain localised to the ankle or radiating along the Achilles tendon; swelling, stiffness, or difficulty bearing
          weight after injury.
      - label: Approach
        value: >-
          Home treatment for mild cases; medical evaluation for severe pain, post-traumatic pain, or symptoms that do not
          settle with time.
  - type: prose
    heading: What ankle pain is
    body: >
      Your ankle is an intricate network of bones, ligaments, tendons and muscles. Strong enough to bear your body weight
      and enable you to move, your ankle can be prone to injury and pain.


      You might feel the pain on the inside or outside of your ankle or along the Achilles tendon, which connects the
      muscles in your lower leg to your heel bone. Although mild ankle pain often responds well to home treatments, it can
      take time to resolve. You should see your doctor for severe ankle pain, especially if it follows an injury.
  - type: prose
    heading: Causes of ankle pain
    body: >
      Injury to any of the ankle bones, ligaments or tendons and several types of arthritis can cause ankle pain. Common
      causes include:


      - Achilles tendinitis

      - Achilles tendon rupture

      - Avulsion fracture

      - Broken foot

      - Bursitis (joint inflammation)

      - Gout (arthritis related to excess uric acid)

      - Osteoarthritis (disease causing the breakdown of joints)

      - Osteochondritis dissecans

      - Plantar fasciitis

      - Pseudogout

      - Psoriatic arthritis

      - Reactive arthritis

      - Rheumatoid arthritis (inflammatory joint disease)

      - Septic arthritis

      - Sprained ankle

      - Stress fractures

      - Tarsal tunnel syndrome
