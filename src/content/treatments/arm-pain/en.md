sections:
  - type: highlights
    items:
      - label: What it is
        value: Pain in the upper limb from joint injury, nerve compression, or referred causes from the neck or heart.
      - label: Symptoms
        value: Aching, sharp or radiating pain; may include numbness, weakness, or swelling depending on cause.
      - label: Approach
        value: Clinical exam to locate the source; imaging and targeted treatment when indicated.
  - type: prose
    heading: Overview
    body: >
      Arm pain can be caused by a wide variety of problems, ranging from joint injuries to compressed nerves. Depending on the cause, arm pain can start suddenly or develop over time.
  - type: prose
    heading: When the problem is elsewhere
    body: >
      In many cases, arm pain actually originates from a problem in your neck or upper spine. Arm pain, particularly pain that radiates into your left arm, can even be a sign of a heart attack.
  - type: prose
    heading: Common causes
    body: |
      Possible causes of arm pain include:

      - Angina (reduced blood flow to the heart)
      - Brachial plexus injury
      - Broken arm
      - Broken wrist
      - Bursitis (joint inflammation)
      - Carpal tunnel syndrome
      - Cervical disk herniation
      - De Quervain's tenosynovitis
      - Dislocated elbow
      - Fibromyalgia
      - Heart attack
      - Rheumatoid arthritis (inflammatory joint disease)
      - Rotator cuff injury
      - Sprains
      - Tendinitis
      - Tennis elbow
      - Thoracic outlet syndrome
      - Ulnar nerve entrapment
