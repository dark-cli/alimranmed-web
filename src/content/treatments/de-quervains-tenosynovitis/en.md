    heading: What De Quervain's tenosynovitis is
    body: >
      De Quervain's tenosynovitis is a painful condition affecting two tendons on the thumb side of the wrist. These
      tendons normally glide smoothly through a small tunnel as you grip, grasp, or pinch. Repetitive hand or wrist
      movements irritate the sheath surrounding them, causing it to thicken and swell — which restricts tendon movement
      and produces pain.


      Although the exact cause is not always clear, chronic overuse of the wrist is the most common factor. Activities
      like gardening, playing golf or racket sports, and lifting a baby are commonly associated with it.


      **Symptoms:**

      - Pain and swelling near the base of the thumb

      - Difficulty gripping or pinching

      - A "sticking" or "stop-and-go" sensation when moving the thumb

      - Pain spreading into the thumb or forearm if untreated


      **Other causes:** Direct injury to the wrist or tendon; inflammatory arthritis such as rheumatoid arthritis.


      **First aid measures:** Immobilise the thumb and wrist with a splint; avoid repetitive thumb movements; apply ice
      to the area.
