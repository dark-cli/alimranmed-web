  - type: prose
    heading: What facet joints are
    body: >
      Facet joints are the small paired joints between two vertebral bones. They sit at the back of the spine and run
      from the neck all the way down to the low back. They can become painful after a [whiplash](/treatments/whiplash/)
      injury of the neck, from arthritis, or from degeneration in the low back. Facet-joint pain is often felt in and
      around the spine and can markedly limit how a patient uses their neck or back.


      When facet joints are painful, bending the spine backward or sideways becomes difficult.
  - type: prose
    heading: How the diagnosis is confirmed
    body: >
      Each facet joint is supplied by medial branch nerves. If these nerves are blocked with local anaesthetic and the
      pain stops, the diagnosis is confirmed — this is the only definitive test that tells the clinician the pain is
      coming from the facet joint itself.


      Once the block is positive (more than 50% pain relief), [radiofrequency
      ablation](/services/radiofrequency/radiofrequency/) of the medial branches provides lasting pain relief.
