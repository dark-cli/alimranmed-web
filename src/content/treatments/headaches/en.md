  - type: prose
    heading: "What chronic headache is"
    body: |
      Chronic daily headache is defined as headache occurring ≥15 days per month for ≥3 months. It includes chronic tension-type headache, chronic migraine, and new daily persistent headache. In many patients, medication overuse (analgesics, triptans, opioids) perpetuates the headache cycle.

  - type: stats_facts
    heading: "What we see in clinic"
    stats:
      - value: "4%"
        label: "Of the global population suffers chronic daily headache"
      - value: "1–2%"
        label: "Have medication-overuse headache (MOH) as the underlying cause"
      - value: "50–60%"
        label: "Improve with migraine prevention therapy alone"
    facts:
      - "Medication-overuse headache develops after >10 days per month of opioids, >15 days of triptans, or >15 days of simple analgesics."
      - "Discontinuation of overused medication triggers temporary worsening before improvement (rebound headache)."
      - "Botulinum toxin is approved for chronic migraine (≥4 migraine days per month) and is given as fixed-dose sets every 12 weeks."

  - type: treatment_groups
    heading: "How it is treated"
    intro: "Identify and eliminate triggers and medication overuse. Escalate preventive therapy. Interventional options for refractory cases."
    groups:
      - title: "Medication discontinuation"
        subtitle: "Overuse-related headache"
        items:
          - "Gradual tapering or abrupt cessation depending on drug"
          - "Expect 4–10 weeks of worsening (rebound) before improvement"
          - "Inpatient detoxification for severe cases"
      - title: "Preventive medication"
        subtitle: "First-line for all chronic headache"
        items:
          - "Beta-blockers (propranolol, timolol)"
          - "Calcium-channel blockers (verapamil, diltiazem)"
          - "Anticonvulsants (topiramate, valproate)"
          - "Tricyclic antidepressants (amitriptyline)"
      - title: "Botulinum toxin"
        subtitle: "Chronic migraine (≥4 migraine days per month)"
        items:
          - "Fixed-dose injections into frontalis, temporalis, occipitalis, neck and shoulder musculature"
          - "Effect develops over 2 weeks, lasts ~12 weeks"
          - "Repeat injections every 12 weeks"
      - title: "Interventional procedures"
        subtitle: "Refractory chronic migraine or tension-type"
        items:
          - "Occipital nerve block or stimulation"
          - "Greater occipital nerve blocks with steroid"
          - "Sphenopalatine ganglion blocks for cluster-like or hemicrania continua patterns"
