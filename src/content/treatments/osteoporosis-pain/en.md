  - type: prose
    heading: "What osteoporosis pain is"
    body: |
      Osteoporosis is a degenerative bone disease that causes bones to become weak or brittle over time, particularly in elderly individuals. As osteoporosis develops, bone mineral density is reduced, making the bones more fragile. Like the rest of the body, our bones are maintained by natural repair functions — as we get older, the body cannot keep up with the rate at which the bones deteriorate.

      For this reason, many patients with osteoporosis will experience frequent injuries, poor posture, and issues with the spine — all of which can cause pain and other symptoms.
  - type: prose
    heading: "How osteoporosis is treated"
    body: |
      Osteoporosis cannot be reversed, but treatments can slow its effects and reduce symptoms. Supplements and medications strengthen the bones and slow the deterioration rate. Physical therapy helps patients improve their strength and posture.

      For pain caused by osteoporosis, the clinic may recommend a range of injection treatments. If a fracture has already occurred in the spine, the clinic will likely recommend **kyphoplasty** — a procedure that injects a specialised cement formula into the affected bones to strengthen them and repair the damage. During your initial visit our doctors will assess your condition to determine which treatment is most effective for your situation.
