    heading: "What sacroiliitis is"
    body: |
      Sacroiliitis is inflammation of one or both sacroiliac joints — situated where the lower spine and pelvis connect. It can cause pain in the buttocks or lower back that extends down one or both legs. Prolonged standing or stair climbing typically makes the pain worse.

      Because its symptoms overlap with other causes of low back pain, sacroiliitis can be difficult to diagnose. It is associated with a group of conditions that cause inflammatory arthritis of the spine.

      **Causes** of sacroiliac joint dysfunction include:

      - **Traumatic injury** — a sudden impact such as a fall or road accident can damage the joints.
      - **Arthritis** — both osteoarthritis and ankylosing spondylitis can affect the sacroiliac joints.
      - **Pregnancy** — the joints must loosen to accommodate childbirth; the added weight and altered gait during pregnancy can cause abnormal wear.
      - **Infection** — the joint can become infected in rare cases.
