  - type: prose
    heading: What tension headaches are
    body: >
      Tension headaches are the most common type of headache. They produce a steady, dull ache — not throbbing —
      affecting both sides of the head and sometimes extending to the back of the neck. Stress and muscle tension in the
      head and neck are major contributing factors, though the exact mechanism is not fully understood.


      **Episodic tension headaches** last 30 minutes to a week and occur fewer than 15 days per month. With time, they
      can transition to the chronic form.


      **Chronic tension headaches** persist for hours and occur 15 or more days per month for at least three months.


      Unlike migraine, tension headaches do not typically cause nausea, vomiting, or visual disturbances. Physical
      activity does not worsen them. Some light or sound sensitivity is possible but is not a defining feature.


      **Self-management** plays an important role: consistent sleep and wake times, daily exercise, regular meals
      (especially breakfast), avoiding personal headache triggers, and stress-reduction practices such as massage or
      yoga.
